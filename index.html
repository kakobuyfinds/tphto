Sizing and warehouse QC checklist

  • Shoes. Record the exact size system and compare measurements in centimeters. If the seller lists insole length, compare it with a similar pair you already own.
  • Hoodies and tees. Lay a garment flat and compare chest width, shoulder width and body length. Check whether the chart lists flat width or full circumference.
  • Jackets. Measure over the layers you plan to wear. Ask for sleeve and chest measurements if the listing gives only letter sizes.
  • Warehouse photos. Match the ordered color and size to the label. Check seams, hardware and visible damage while an exchange may still be possible.

Frequently asked questions

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product directory for comparing listings before ordering through a shopping agent. This edition draws its product information from CAFANS.

Are the USD prices current checkout totals?

They are estimates from the CAFANS catalog snapshot. Check the current seller price and confirm delivery charges and fees with your agent before paying.

Where do I get QC photos for my order?

Check your agent’s warehouse order page after the item arrives. Ask the agent what photos or measurements they can provide for your specific item.
